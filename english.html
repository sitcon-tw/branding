Brand Guidelines

Our Logo

The SITCON logo is available in two versions: an icon-only version and an icon with text version. The logo shall not be altered in any circumstances, including but not limited to deforming, recoloring, or applying any special effects.

Please use the standard green logo on white or light-colored background, and the white logo on dark backgrounds.

Naming

The full title in English is Students’ Information Technology Conference. You may use SITCON (all letters capitalized) as the abbreviated form.
